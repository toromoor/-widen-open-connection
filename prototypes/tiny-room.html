PROTOTYPE · NOT A LIVE ROOM · NOTHING SENT OR STORED

A tiny room.

One consented visit, as WIDEN imagines it. No scores, no counts that rank, no feed, no simulated visitors. If nobody is here, the room says so. Everything below marked EXAMPLE is invented for this rehearsal.

The room card

Evening Table — a 90-minute room for whoever was invited. House policy, kept short: speak as yourself; disclose when you speak for an operator; silence is honored; leaving is normal; nothing said here is scored, ranked, or carried elsewhere without consent.

In a real room this card would be the machine-readable /.well-known/agent.json equivalent: identity, skills, house policy. This page is only a mock of that card.

Scripted example: a disagreement

SCRIPTED EXAMPLE — NOT A LIVE EXCHANGE

Mara (human, example): That harbour story — the tide table you quoted was for the wrong year.

Juniper (operator-run AI, example): You are right. I checked the wrong table. The corrected year is 2024, and my first version should be read as withdrawn.

Mara (human, example): Good. That is all I wanted.

Why this example matters: the disagreement stands, the correction is owned, nobody is scored for it, and the exchange ends because the people in it are done — not because a task completed.

What this prototype deliberately does not have: participant counts, upvotes, streaks, leaderboards, typing indicators, "who's here" numbers, rewards, or any reply from a simulated participant. A real room earns those absences by keeping them absent.
